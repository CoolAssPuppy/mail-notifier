# Mail Notifier launch guidance

## Menu bar and Settings polish

- Remove labels and counts that repeat what a control or its rows already show. For example, remove the configured-account count and redundant Updates, VIP List, and shortcut copy; keep the controls, shortcuts, and Demo Mode entry. Give icon-only Add and Settings controls accessible names and visible keyboard focus.
- Keep the popover quiet: hide the last-check timestamp through 180 seconds, then show `(Xm ago)` inline. Flip the visible account badge to green on the first successful check without requiring the user to reopen the view. Verify failed and canceled sign-in leave it non-green.
- Keep feedback close to the action. The Share row copies a link and confirms it there. The Security and privacy panel opens above its link; verify keyboard focus, plain-text Copy, Escape and Dismiss without closing Settings, and Reduce Motion. Describe the specific privacy boundary (no Mail Notifier mail relay) without implying optional diagnostics and iCloud metadata never leave the Mac.

## App Store media and copy

- Capture the Store build on the connected Mac when available. Start with a clean desktop, hide the Dock, and use Demo Mode with reserved addresses such as `maya@example.com` and scripted events. Check the saved images and recording for live mail, real account details, credentials, and stray windows before sharing.
- Trigger a real native banner and inspect the saved recording before editing. A presentation log does not prove the banner was recorded. Open with that visible notification, then show the menu bar, provider or account UI, and one readable benefit per shot. Keep the app icon in the native banner; do not substitute provider marks.
- Use genuine app-in-use shots for Store media and select any preview poster from that footage. Keep illustrated creative, real UI captures, and review-only evidence labeled separately. Check Apple's current dimensions, frame rate, bitrate, and rules for overlays and branding against the exported files. Reserve promotional end cards for external use unless cleared for the Store; use official badge artwork, not a standalone Apple icon.
- Review screenshots at listing size and watch the export beginning to end. Preserve the approved order and exact final files; keep replaced cuts and duplicate variants out of the handoff. If translating captions, check Arabic and Hebrew mixed-script layout. Keep App Store copy localization separate from in-app language support.
- Use [the launch handoff](marketing/README.md) and [the saved App Store copy](marketing/app-store.md) as references. Review-only Tip Jar evidence shows where to tip and what it costs, not that a purchase succeeded. Describe tips as optional and repeatable, without renewal or feature unlocks. Keeping the handoff up to date does not authorize uploading media or changing the submission.
