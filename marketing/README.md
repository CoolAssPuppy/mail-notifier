These are the exact files the owner approved to try first. Apple acceptance and media ingestion were not verified. The five captioned PNGs are 2560 × 1600; keep this order:

1. [Native notifications](submission-candidates/01-native-notifications.png): illustrated banners, not a native capture.
2. [Gmail and Outlook](submission-candidates/02-gmail-and-outlook.png): provider marks, with no running app UI.
3. [Unlimited accounts](submission-candidates/03-unlimited-accounts.png): illustrated sample notifications.

5. [Direct mail checks](submission-candidates/05-direct-mail-checks.png): enlarged illustration of the menu-bar popover, not an unedited capture.

[Stylized badge cut](submission-candidates/mac-app-preview-stylized.mp4): 25.6 seconds, 1920 × 1080, H.264 at 30 fps with stereo AAC, ending on the official Mac App Store badge. The notifications are illustrated. The end card, illustrated footage and approximately 0.28 Mbps overall bitrate may not meet App Preview requirements. The same cut is the only finished promotional edit; there is no separate website or social cut. There is no approved poster or localized media set. Confirm provider-mark rights before publishing marked assets.

The account examples in slide 5 and the badge cut are from the app's [public Demo Mode fixtures](../Source/Services/Providers/DemoProvider.swift); the messages are scripted. Use reserved example-domain addresses for future captures and recuts. For an Apple-compliant recut, start on an actual captured native banner, show the running app, and omit the promotional end card. The rejected native-capture draft did not show the banner and is excluded.
